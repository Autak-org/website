EN
Use your talent and acquire new experiences
Our team is constantly evolving and relies on people like you! Share your personal experience and contribute to improving our products and thus empowering our users.
Open positions
Social media
Inclusion is a global long-standing issue. We want to reach people with our projects. Furthermore, our team is constantly looking for new people, both to meet new user requirements and to replace departing members.
You will learn how to present a project/product and how to plan and execute social campaigns.
3d modeling
We will create the 3D models for the load-bearing elements, fasteners, and control elements.
You will learn how to create models with Fusion 360 and FreeCAD, how to 3D print them, and how to prepare the models for presentation.
Microcontroller programming
We use ESP32 chips to control the wheelchair wheels, read the speed, the user interface, and tilt the seat.
You will learn how to work on firmware in an international team, how firmware integrates into a larger project, and gain experience with ESP32, CAN bus, and 45-volt high-torque motors.
Construction
The wheelchair is assembled from numerous milled aluminum parts, screws, tubes, and plastic components. We need a second prototype for presentations, testing, and trials.
You will learn how to assemble a wheelchair and manufacture its individual parts.
Continuous integration programming
To guarantee quality and take our team and our products to the next level, we need automation in all areas.
You need prior programming knowledge. You will gain experience integrating automation into all areas of a wheelchair-building team.
Process management
Our team consists of a core team and a part that comes and goes. To achieve long-term progress, new people need to settle in quickly. To ensure their work flows smoothly into the final product, we need you!
Are you interested in management roles but have little or no experience? Come and try us out!
